PaleoSynth privacy policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Developer: Myster E Studios

Contact: Adam.ellis0@icloud.com

Summary

PaleoSynth doesn't collect any data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't send your gameplay or personal data to us or to anyone else. It doesn't connect to the internet to play.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress, a backup of it, and a copy of your previous institute on your device. It also remembers a few settings there, such as whether sound effects are on. These files may be included in device or iCloud backups that you manage with Apple. Deleting the app deletes the files stored on the device.

Notifications

If you choose to turn them on, the game asks iOS to show a notification when a timer finishes, such as an incubation, a sequencing run or a conservation programme. These notifications are scheduled on your device by iOS. No notification server is involved, and they contain no personal data. You can turn them off in the game's Research records or in the Settings app.

Photos and sharing

When a species is restored, you can share its discovery card or save it to your photo library through the system share sheet. The game only adds the image you chose. It can't see or read your photos. Anything you share goes only where you send it.

Other websites

Species knowledge pages link to sources such as the Natural History Museum, Wikipedia, museums and universities. They open outside the game, and those websites have their own privacy policies.

Apple

Apple may give developers aggregated App Store statistics, and crash reports from people who have agreed to share them, under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The game doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

Any changes will be published at this address with a new effective date.
