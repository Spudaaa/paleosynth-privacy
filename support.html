Get in touch

For help, bug reports or ideas, email Adam.ellis0@icloud.com. It helps to include your device, your iOS version and the version of the game, which is shown on the About screen.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. The game saves on your device as you play and keeps a backup. If the save is ever damaged, the game offers to restore the backup. Your progress is included in your device or iCloud backup, so it comes back if you restore your iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Do timers keep running when the game is closed?

Yes. Incubation, sequencing and other timers carry on while the game is closed, and the welcome-back report shows what finished.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Open Research records in the game and use the notifications switch. You can also change them in the Settings app under PaleoSynth.

How do I turn sound effects off?

Open Research records in the game and use the sound effects switch.

Does the game need the internet?

No. It plays fully offline. Only the links on species knowledge pages open websites.

PaleoSynth doesn't collect any data. Read the privacy policy.
